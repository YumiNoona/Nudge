Why Nudge

Mindful money,
minus the spreadsheets.

📲

Zero-friction capture

Bank SMS and UPI notifications are parsed on-device. Your transactions appear automatically — no typing, no CSV wrangling.

🎮

Playful, never preachy

Earn XP, unlock 42 badges, complete weekly challenges. Streak freezes mean one missed day won't reset everything. Encouragement, not guilt.

🔐

Privacy by default

All parsing happens on-device. Nothing is uploaded. The database is encrypted with AES-256. Your data lives where it belongs — with you.

💍

Smart budget rings

Real-time progress rings on your home screen. They shift color gently as you spend — coral when close, never alarming red.

📅

Spend calendar heatmap

See your spending intensity across the month in a GitHub-contribution-style grid. Patterns become obvious at a glance.

🔄

Sync without spying

Optional E2E encrypted sync between Android and Web. The relay server stores only encrypted blobs it cannot read. Self-hostable.

How it works

Three steps to clarity.

1. Connect & capture

Grant SMS access once — Nudge reads only bank and UPI messages, entirely on your device. Transactions appear automatically with smart categorization. A single swipe confirms or corrects the category. That's it.
