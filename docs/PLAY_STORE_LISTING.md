# Google Play listing copy

## App name

**Nudge: Expense Manager**

## Short description

**Private expense tracking from bank alerts, statements, receipts and manual entry.**

## Full description

Nudge is a calm, local-first expense manager that turns everyday payment activity into a clean, editable timeline.

Capture bank and UPI transaction alerts on your device, add expenses manually, import supported statements, or scan a receipt. Nudge organizes the result into useful monthly analytics without requiring an account or uploading your financial history to a Nudge server.

AUTOMATIC, WITH YOUR PERMISSION

• Recognize supported bank, card and UPI transaction SMS alerts
• Recognize payment notifications from supported apps
• Review uncertain matches before they affect your records
• Learn local merchant corrections and reject repeated non-transactions
• Prevent duplicate captures and repeated statement imports

FAST MANUAL TRACKING

• Purpose-built amount keypad with haptic feedback
• Expense, income and refund entries
• Custom accounts and categories
• Searchable icon library and custom category symbols

IMPORT AND SCAN

• Import supported PDF, text, CSV and image statements
• Preview transactions before saving
• Scan receipts with an embedded on-device camera flow
• On-device OCR for supported documents and images

UNDERSTAND YOUR SPENDING

• Monthly transaction timeline
• Expense mix by category
• Daily spending rhythm and key totals
• Responsive home-screen widgets

LOCAL-FIRST PRIVACY

• No Nudge account required
• No ads or analytics SDK
• Financial parsing and learning run on your device
• Optional saved transaction sources are encrypted with Android Keystore
• Export, delete, or reset your data whenever you choose

Automatic capture is optional. Nudge remains fully usable for manual expense tracking when SMS, notification or camera access is declined.
